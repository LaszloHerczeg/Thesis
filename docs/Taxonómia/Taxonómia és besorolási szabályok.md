# Hungarian Legal Case Categorization Rules

Oct 11, 2026 · @László

Six Hungarian legal fields can be told apart from a non-lawyer's description, because people almost always say **who the other side is** and **what happened**. The rules below classify on those two facts and treat keywords only as evidence for them.

## The six categories

Ask the decisive questions top-down; the first "yes" is the primary category.

| # | Category | Decisive question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Büntetőjog**: criminal & petty offences | Is there an act that is punishable in itself (violence or threats of violence, theft, deliberate damage, a scam by a fake or vanished party)? Or is the person already in a police case as suspect, victim or witness? |
| 2 | **Öröklési jog**: inheritance | Has someone died (or is the person planning a will)? Is the question who gets what they left, or who pays their debts? |
| 3 | **Családjog**: family | Is the other side a spouse, partner, ex or the child's other parent? Is the issue the breakup, the children, support, or property they built up together? |
| 4 | **Munkajog**: employment | Is the other side the person's employer (or employee)? Is the issue the job: pay, dismissal, hours, leave, safety or treatment at work? |
| 5 | **Ingatlan és lakásbérlet**: property & tenancy | Is the issue owning, renting, buying, selling or using a flat, house or land? Is it with a landlord, tenant, neighbour, the condominium or the other side of a property sale? |
| 6 | **Fogyasztóvédelem**: consumer | Did the person, as a private individual, buy or subscribe to something from a business? Is the problem with that product or service? |

### How to apply

- The first "yes" is the primary category.
- Any later "yes" becomes a secondary label. Domestic violence is Criminal + Family. Money stolen from a bank account through phishing is Criminal + Consumer.
- The order matters:
    - A crime can happen inside any relationship.
    - A death outranks the family and property details around it.
    - A breakup decides what happens to the shared flat.
    - Renting or buying a home from a company is still a property matter.
- The rules are role-neutral. A landlord asking about a tenant lands in the same category as a tenant asking about a landlord.

## Signals and exclusions

Each category lists the lay phrases that point to it and the cases that belong elsewhere.

### 1. Büntetőjog: Criminal (Btk., Szabs. tv.)

- Signals:
    - Violence or threats: *megvert, megütött, megfenyegetett, zaklat*
    - Property taken or damaged: *ellopták, betörtek, kiraboltak, megrongálta*
    - A scam by a fake or vanished party: *átvertek és eltűnt, kamu webshop, adathalász, leemelték a pénzt a számlámról*
    - Being in a case: *idézést kaptam a rendőrségre, gyanúsítottként hallgattak ki, tanúként idéztek be, szabálysértés, ittasan vezettem*
- Send elsewhere: *lopás, csalás, átvert* used about a known party who simply won't pay, refund or give something back. Examples are a landlord keeping the deposit, a shop refusing a refund, or an employer withholding wages. Classify those by the relationship.

### 2. Öröklési jog: Inheritance (Ptk. Book 7)

- Signals: a death plus assets, debts or a will. Examples: *meghalt az apám / anyám / a férjem, hagyaték, örökség, végrendelet, hagyatéki tárgyalás, közjegyző, köteles rész, kitagadott, özvegyi jog, lemondanék az örökségről, a testvérem mindent magának akar*.
- Send elsewhere:
    - How the person died → Criminal or Other.
    - A survivor's pension → Other.
    - A living parent handing over property now (*átíratja, ajándékozza*) → Property.
    - An inherited flat whose probate is already closed, where the heirs now argue about using or selling it → Property.

### 3. Családjog: Family (Ptk. Book 4)

- Signals: the other side is a partner or the other parent (*a férjem, a feleségem, az élettársam, a volt párom, a gyerek apja / anyja*). The topic is the breakup or the children: *válás, különköltöztünk, gyerektartás, tartásdíj, láthatás, nem engedi látni a gyereket, kinél legyen a gyerek, felügyeleti jog, vagyonmegosztás, apaság, gyámhivatal*.
- Send elsewhere:
    - Violence by a family member → Criminal, with Family as secondary.
    - A relative's death → Inheritance.
    - Ordinary deals between relatives, like a loan to a brother or a shared business → Other.

### 4. Munkajog: Employment (Mt.)

- Signals: *kirúgtak, felmondtak, a főnököm, a munkáltatóm, nem fizették ki a fizetésemet / túlórát, szabadság, próbaidő, munkaszerződés, végkielégítés, felmondási idő, kilépő papírok, nem jelentettek be, feketén dolgoztam, munkabaleset, mobbing*.
- Decide by how the work is described, not by the contract's label. A boss giving instructions, fixed hours and regular pay mean Employment, even if it was called *megbízás* or invoiced. Public-sector staff and employers asking about their employees also belong here.
- Send elsewhere:
    - Independent jobs for clients who don't direct the work → Other.
    - Pensions and unemployment benefits → Other.

### 5. Ingatlan és lakásbérlet: Property (Ptk., Lakástörvény, Társasházi törvény)

- Signals:
    - Renting: *albérlet, főbérlő, bérlő, kaució, bérleti szerződés, ki akar tenni, kilakoltatás*
    - Neighbours and the building: *szomszéd, zajong, telekhatár, kerítés, átlóg a fa, beázott a lakás a felső szomszédtól, társasház, közös képviselő, közös költség*
    - Buying or owning: *lakásvásárlás, adásvételi szerződés, foglaló, rejtett hiba, tulajdoni lap, haszonélvezet*
- Send elsewhere:
    - The home in a breakup → Family.
    - An inherited home before probate closes → Inheritance.
    - Renovation, repairs or a utility bill → Consumer, because the other side is a business selling a service.
    - Housing that comes with a job → Employment.

### 6. Fogyasztóvédelem: Consumer (Ptk. Book 6 + consumer-protection rules)

- Signals: a purchase or subscription (*vettem, rendeltem a webshopból, előfizetés*) plus a problem (*hibás, elromlott, nem jött meg a csomag, nem veszik vissza, garancia, jótállás, csere, visszaküldtem, visszakérem a pénzem, szerviz, túlszámláztak, késett / törölték a járatot*). The other side is a shop, webshop, repairer, builder, utility, telecom provider, bank, insurer, airline or travel agency.
- Send elsewhere:
    - A private seller (second-hand, marketplace) → Other, or Criminal if they deceived the buyer and vanished.
    - A purchase for the person's own business → Other.
    - A property purchase or a landlord → Property.
    - A debt already in collection (*fizetési meghagyás, végrehajtó, behajtó cég*) → Other.

## Trap words

Some everyday words show up in several fields, so a keyword alone should never decide.

| Word | Could be | Decide by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *felmondtak* | Employment or Property | Was a job or a rental terminated? |
| *bérlet* | Property (lease) or Consumer (gym or transit pass) | Is it a home or a membership? |
| *lakás, ház* | Family, Inheritance, Property, Consumer | The other side and the trigger, never the word |
| *fenyeget, feljelentem, rendőrség* | Criminal, or pressure in a civil dispute | Criminal only for violence, threats of violence or an actual police case. *"Azzal fenyeget, hogy beperel / elviszi a gyereket"* is classified by its topic |
| *rezsi* | Property or Consumer | Did the bill come from the landlord or the utility company? |
| *a férjem, a párom* | Family, or a bystander in another field | Family only if the issue is between the two of them: the relationship, the children or splitting assets |

Some words carry no category signal at all: *szerződés, per, beperelem, ügyvéd, jogaim, kártérítés*.

## When nothing fits

Label it **Egyéb / nem egyértelmű** (Other / unclear) in two situations:

- **No question gets a "yes".** Typical cases are dealings with authorities (taxes, permits, fines, benefits, pensions), debt collection and enforcement, deals between private individuals, and insurance or traffic-damage claims.
- **The text names neither the other side nor what happened.** Don't guess here; ask one follow-up: "Who is the problem with, and what happened?"

Debt enforcement and dealings with authorities are left out of the six on purpose. They overlap with the others: a debt can come from a bank loan, unpaid rent or child support. So they don't separate cleanly from a lay description.
